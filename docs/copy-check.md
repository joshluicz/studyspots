## Self-check (ai-tells.md)
1. **Bans searched** across all 183 distinct visible strings in the built pages (`scripts` sweep, 2026-10-30): no contrast
   reveal, no negation list, no colon reveal, no em or en dash, no stock opener, no exclamation mark, none of the cap
   vocabulary (seamless, robust, powerful, unlock, streamline, curated, discover, effortless).
2. **Sentences over 20 words**: only verbatim operator quotes in "Where this comes from" (kept as read) and three meta
   descriptions, each one plain clause plus one.
3. **Caps**: one fragment on the home page ("No account."), no reflexive threes outside the three-step "How this works".
4. **Read aloud**: the area lede and the spot notes read as a person telling a friend where to sit.
5. **Swap test**: the headline alone fails it and is kept as the category line; the subhead and the three "How this works"
   lines carry the difference and no competitor could paste them.
6. **Second-generation tells**: no tidy closers; section lengths differ; the only "wisdom-shaped" sentence candidate
   ("When nobody has, the page says so.") is a verifiable statement about the page.

## Claims declined, with their unlock
- "Open now" as a filter: declined, because it would print an hour the site computed rather than one a page states.
  Unlock: structured hours per weekday from each operator page, checked by a person.
- Distance or walking minutes from the station: declined; only operator-stated station names are shown
  ("per the operator's page"). Unlock: a measured route per spot.
- Any socket number: declined by the brief. Unlock: a person's report on the live site.
