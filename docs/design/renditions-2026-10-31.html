Thesis. Every fact sits in a hard box. Borders and offset shadows do the grouping, so the eye never has to guess what belongs to what.

Bets on students trust a page that looks hand-built and unpolished over one that looks like an app store listing.

Costs about 14px of border and shadow per card edge; seven cards burn a third of the screen on chrome.

Principle. “Build hierarchy with, in order of strength: size → weight → colour/contrast → space → position”

Honest risk. The block shadow is a costume. It is loud for a tool whose whole value is quiet accuracy, and the crowd will have seen it on every Gumroad clone this year.

Built by Eitri Brokkr

Thesis. One material, pressed and raised. Cards are the paper itself lifted by light, so nothing on the page is a different colour from anything else except the one blue action.

Bets on a calm, single-material surface is what a student wants to look at for the eighth hour of the day.

Costs hierarchy lives entirely in shadows, so it costs two shadows per element and reads flat in a screenshot.

Principle. “Gestalt shortcuts: group related items with proximity; separate sections with space, not lines”

Honest risk. A card edge made of shadow has no measurable contrast. WCAG 1.4.11 wants 3:1 for component boundaries and this style cannot deliver it, so on a dim phone or in sunlight the list collapses into one beige field.

Thesis. The facts float on frosted panes over a soft, coloured ground. Depth comes from blur and translucency rather than lines or shadows.

Bets on a student picks the page that feels like their phone's own UI, and most of them are on iOS.

Costs backdrop-filter on seven cards is the most expensive paint in this set; a mid-range Android will drop frames on scroll.

Principle. “Avoid pure #000 and pure #FFF for large fields”

Honest risk. Text contrast depends on what happens to be behind the glass at that scroll position. The numbers below are the worst case over the two blobs; a photo or a darker blob later would break them without anyone changing a token. It is also the look taste-skill lists first among LLM defaults.

Thesis. Flat colour blocks, squiggles and confetti dots. Each kind of place gets a fill, so a student can tell a library from a mall before reading a word.

Bets on students share things that look like a sticker sheet; a screenshot of this page would travel on Telegram.

Costs three extra fills plus two patterns, every one of which competes with the status colours for meaning.

Principle. “Structure: one dominant (here: near-black/charcoal), one accent (warm gold/amber), a small neutral ramp. That's it.”

Honest risk. Pattern noise competes with the facts, and the status colours (green reported, amber partly known) lose their meaning next to three decorative fills. Muted grey and blue both fail on the pink fill, so every word on a fill has to be ink; the style forces one colour of type.
