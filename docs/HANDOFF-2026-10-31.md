## Known limits of v0
- Suggestions have no moderation UI: delete by editing the KV value `s:<slug>` in the Cloudflare dashboard.
- KV allows 1,000 writes a day on the free tier; each suggestion is two writes.
- "Open now" is deliberately not a filter: it would print an hour the site computed rather than one a page states.
